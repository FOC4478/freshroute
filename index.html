01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The journey from
farm to table should
work better.

Fresh produce moves through multiple layers of the agricultural supply chain before reaching the businesses that need it.

FreshRoute is designed to simplify that journey by connecting farmers and food businesses through a coordinated sourcing, ordering and delivery platform.

Explore the opportunity ↗
02 / THE PROBLEM

A broken connection
between farms and businesses.

Food businesses need consistent access to fresh produce. Farmers need reliable buyers. The existing supply chain often makes both sides work harder than necessary.

01
🚚

Unpredictable Supply

Restaurants and retailers can struggle to source consistent quantities of fresh produce when they need it.

02
💸

Too Many Middlemen

Multiple intermediaries can increase costs and reduce the value farmers receive for their produce.

03
🥬

Post-Harvest Waste

Delays, poor coordination and limited market access can contribute to produce losses.

04
📦

Fragmented Logistics

Sourcing, quality checks and transportation are often handled separately, creating operational challenges.

The opportunity: Build a more coordinated connection between agricultural producers and the businesses that depend on them.
